## Observable acceptance
Legacy settings are untouched/unused, fresh defaults enabled/display/not-quiet apply, valid new unknown fields survive and invalid/unreadable files remain write-protected. Ordinary serialized atomic saves and a basic save failure are honest; no concurrent-writer or partial-write recovery campaign. stop overrides even gates temporarily, start rearms rather than forcing idle wakefulness, disable persists, and duplicate snapshots do not count as new acquisitions. Cover settings and control behavior before binding real inhibitors.
